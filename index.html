01 — WHO WE ARE

Energy supply that keeps business moving.

Felix Petroleum is an emerging oil and gas trading and distribution company based in Rivers State, Nigeria, built around one promise: dependable products, competitive pricing and service customers can plan around.

Our model connects procurement, storage and distribution with efficient logistics — helping businesses across the Niger Delta meet operational energy needs without costly interruptions. We are growing to become the leading independent petroleum distributor in southern Nigeria.
